How this view relates to the observations

    Recurrence compares each revolution with its immediate predecessor at matching orbital phases. S sums duration × continuous similarity. The first revolution supplies an initial reference. Time inside the radial region and recurrence are different measurements. No new simulations, model calls or credit updates are performed by this viewer.
