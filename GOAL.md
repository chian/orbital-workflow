## Episode, credit and continuation ownership

Use the real iterative Episode runtime. Children own sub-investigations rather
than single prescribed tool operations. There is no compulsory branch order,
minimum branch attendance, private scientific search loop or arbitrary turn cap.
Refinements remain within the assigned investigation. Only numerical yield
continuation stops a scientific Episode; completing an experiment or filling a
form does not introduce another stopping rule.

Keep score assignment, yield estimation and continuation separate. Credit uses
actual recorded score improvements; parent credit projects machine observations
rather than importing child totals. Same-target delegation inherits known progress
without an acquired observation or fresh credit for the same saved result.
Different scientific targets can have independent credit. Do not infer scientific
target identity from labels or manufacture a new knowledge/support score.

Supply one marginal-credit observation per completed local acquisition. A child's
many internal acquisitions make one completed action observation for its parent.
The estimator owns fitting/history/window/uncertainty. No controller smoothing,
EWMA, forecast memory, decay, artificial yield alteration or additional stop rule.
There are no scientific acceptance/rejection gates or proof prerequisites.
